About Sof Omar Cave

Sof Omar Cave is a spectacular limestone cave system in southeastern Ethiopia, located in the East Bale Zone of the Oromia Region. It is the longest cave in Ethiopia, with a mapped length of about 15.1 km and 42 entrances and exits.

Photo Gallery

Key Features

  • Carved by the Weyib River through Jurassic limestone
  • River sinks at Ayyo Makko and resurfaces at Holuca
  • Named after Sheikh Sof Omar, an Islamic holy man
  • Important Islamic pilgrimage and cultural site

Plan Your Visit

  • Access from Robe town (~110 km, 2-hour drive by 4WD)
  • About 540 km from Addis Ababa
  • 2 km walking route inside the cave (~2 hours)
  • Local guide required — around 300 ETB
  • Warning: Never enter during heavy rain (flash flood risk).
